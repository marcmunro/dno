12.1. 0.9.x Series

These releases are the beta test series and the run-up to it.

12.1.1. 0.9.4

Improved unit test handling for libraries: uses the libraries' own Makefiles if they exist.

Plus many minor tweaks.

12.1.2. 0.9.3

First release that is considered truly usable. Fully functional, with unit tests to prove it.

Includes a major refactoring of dno, plus addition of unit testing framework and tests.

12.1.3. Earlier

Experimental releases. Mostly usable.
